# Images Folder

## Profile Photo

Place your profile photo here as `profile.jpg` (or `profile.png`)

**Requirements:**
- **Filename:** `profile.jpg` or `profile.png`
- **Size:** Recommended 500x500px or larger (square format)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Quality:** High quality, well-lit professional photo
- **Background:** Clean background preferred

The image will be automatically:
- Cropped to a circle (180px diameter on desktop)
- Given a green border matching your brand color
- Displayed at the top of your homepage

## Favicon

Place your favicon here as `favicon.svg` (or `favicon.png`)

**Recommended:**
- SVG format for best quality
- Simple icon/logo design
- Size: 32x32px or vector SVG
